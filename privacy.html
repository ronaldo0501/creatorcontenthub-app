CreatorContentHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

CreatorContentHub ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

CreatorContentHub collects only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information provided via Sign in with Apple
  • Content drafts, calendar events, and shoot concepts you create
  • Calendar data if you grant permission to sync with iPhone Calendar
  • Subscription status via Apple's App Store

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not collect social media credentials or passwords
  • We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites
  • We do not store social media platform tokens on our servers

Data Storage

Your content data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Calendar sync uses Apple's EventKit and stores data in your personal iCloud Calendar.

Sign in with Apple

We use Sign in with Apple for authentication. Apple manages your credentials — we only receive a unique user identifier and optionally your name and email if you choose to share them.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

CreatorContentHub is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the app.
