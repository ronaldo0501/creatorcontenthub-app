Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my social platforms?

Go to Settings → Connected Platforms and tap Connect next to each platform. Note that some platforms have API limitations — the app will show you exactly what's available for each one.

How do I sync with my iPhone Calendar?

Go to the Calendar tab and tap "Enable" on the sync banner at the top. You'll be asked to grant calendar permission — tap "Allow Full Access" to enable two-way sync.

What is Demo Mode?

Demo Mode loads a sample creator profile so you can explore all features without connecting any accounts. Toggle it on or off in Settings → Demo Mode.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → CreatorContentHub and tap Cancel Subscription.

My data disappeared after reinstalling — what happened?

Content drafts, shoot concepts, and tasks are stored locally on your device. Reinstalling the app clears local data. We recommend using the Calendar sync feature to preserve scheduled events in iCloud.

Which platforms have full analytics support?

YouTube has the most complete API access and provides full analytics. Instagram, TikTok, and X have varying levels of API access — the app clearly shows what's available for each platform and provides direct links to open those apps when needed.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → tap your subscription status → Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be restored automatically if it's still active.
